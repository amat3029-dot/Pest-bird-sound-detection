SOUND & NOTIFICATIONS

Smart alert settings

Bird pop-up notifications appear only when the selected siren is approved for laptop playback or an Arduino buzzer command is queued. Other detections remain in history. The species cooldown prevents repeated sirens and matching pop-ups.

Laptop siren loudness is limited by your computer's system volume and speakers. Set alert volume and system volume to a comfortable level; very loud audio can harm hearing.

Alert sounds require browser interaction before playback.
