Identification Information

Bird Guard AI uses BirdNET to analyse recorded audio and identify possible bird species.

Bird identification confidence represents the model's confidence in its species prediction. It does not represent crop damage probability.

Crop Pest Monitoring

Review documented relationships between bird species and agricultural crops.

Crop Assessment Information

The system compares identified bird species against its crop knowledge database.

  • Documented pest relationships
  • Known types of crop damage
  • Affected crop growth stages
  • Research and evidence sources

Unknown relationships remain classified as Unknown. A historical damage percentage is not a live pest-risk percentage.
